## Corporate support and specialist consulting

**DNA Tech** offers dedicated corporate services for organizations using
hbBridge or operating TOTVS Protheus environments:

- **Advanced support and architecture:** complex incidents in legacy
  customizations and architectural guidance.
- **Database and routine tuning:** query optimization, lock reduction and
  performance of critical routines on SQL Server, Oracle and PostgreSQL.
- **REST integration and modernization:** APIs, automation and migration to TL++.
- **Engagements:** monthly retainers with SLA or fixed-scope projects.
